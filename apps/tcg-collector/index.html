TCG Collector

Organize your trading card collection and build decks, all in one place. TCG Collector currently supports Star Wars: Unlimited, with more card games planned.

For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro.

Features

  • The full card catalog

    Browse every released set with card images and full details, including both sides of leader cards. After the first download, browsing works offline.

  • Your collection

    Keep track of which cards you own and how many copies of each. Add cards by searching, browsing a set, or scanning them with your camera.

  • Deck building

    Build decks from the cards you own. The app keeps each deck legal, shows its cost curve, and can help fill it in.

  • Deck notes

    Write notes for any deck. On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can suggest deck names and summarize your notes, all on the device.

  • Search everything

    Find cards, sets, and decks from one place, and filter by aspect, cost, trait, keyword, and more.

  • Widget and iCloud

    Put a favorite deck on your Home Screen. If you want, turn on iCloud Sync to keep your collection and decks the same on all your devices.

Privacy

TCG Collector has no accounts, ads, or analytics. Your collection and decks stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, which stores them in your own private iCloud account.
